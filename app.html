Phone vibration

Scan the QR code with your phone camera. The phone vibrates as you explore the map.

The phone opens this site over the internet: it doesn't need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Can't see the QR code? Copy the link and send it to your phone.

Phone won't connect?
  1. The page doesn't open on the phone: use the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network), allow the port in the firewall, or choose Internet: GitHub Pages above.
  2. It opens but keeps “Looking for the computer”: generate a new QR code, or try another network or mobile data.
  3. It connects but doesn't vibrate: tap “Turn on vibration” on the phone. iPhone/Safari can't vibrate on the web, so the receiver only pulses on screen.
